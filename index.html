Why I started this

I started Bridge to China for my cousin.

My cousin lives in China and spends hours outside of school studying English. Bridge to China connects families like hers with volunteers in America for free, one-on-one conversation practice, a more authentic alternative to apps and textbooks. Volunteers benefit too, earning verified service hours for their time.

Our story

Why Bridge to China exists.

Watching my cousin put in real effort outside of school just to keep up with English made me think about how many other families in China are doing the same thing, often paying for extra lessons or relying on apps that can't hold a real conversation.

Bridge to China connects those families with volunteers in America for free. Students get authentic, one-on-one conversation practice with a native speaker instead of a textbook or an app. Volunteers get something back too: verified service hours for the time they give.
