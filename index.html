Our mission
"We believe language builds friendships, cultural understanding, and opportunity — and that the simplest way to change a life is to show up for one conversation a week."

Bridge to China was founded to close the gap between students in China who want real conversational English practice, and native and fluent English speakers around the world who want their time to matter. Every session is free for students and every hour is verified for volunteers.

Frequently asked

Good to know before you start.

Yes. It's always free for students, and there's never a cost for volunteers to teach.

Any fluent English speaker aged 18 or older who completes our short orientation and background review can volunteer.

No formal experience is required. Orientation covers everything you need to lead a friendly, effective conversation session.

Hours are logged automatically after each completed lesson, then reviewed and approved by our administrators before certificates are issued.

Students range from young learners to working adults, grouped by age band during registration so matches and content stay age-appropriate.

A computer or phone with a camera, microphone, and stable internet connection is all you need — sessions run right in the browser.
