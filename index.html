Trust & safety

A bridge worth trusting, on both ends.

Background-checked volunteers

Every volunteer passes an identity and background check before their first match.

Guardian consent for minors

Students under 18 register with a parent or guardian, who can view schedules and messages at any time.

Everything stays on-platform

Lessons, messages, and calls happen only inside My English Tutor. We never move conversations to personal phone numbers or outside apps.

Recorded, reviewable sessions

Lessons can be recorded for quality and safety review by our team.

Moderated messaging

All messages are screened for respectful, on-topic conversation.

Easy reporting

Anyone can flag a concern in one tap, and our team responds quickly.

About us

Why we built the bridge.

My English Tutor started with a simple observation. Millions of students want to speak English with confidence, and just as many native speakers have an hour a week and a genuine interest in the world. Conversation is the missing piece that no app, textbook, or grammar drill can replace, and volunteering is one of the most direct ways to make an international impact.

We connect the two, safely and simply, so language can do what it has always done: build friendships, open doors, and bring people closer together.
